          How to Use

          Install App

          • Use Add to Home Screen / Install App to use it as a PWA (faster, own icon, device app lock).
          • Tapping install button after open playstore then app automatically installed in your mobile

          Reminders

          • Reminders page → ＋ Add New Reminder → Title, Start date, End date → Save.
          • Edit with ✏️, delete with 🗑️. Today and tomorrow due reminders show on Home.

          Notes

          • Notes page → ＋ Add New Note → write text, optionally attach a file or tick table format → Save.
          • Saved date and time are added automatically and shown with each note, also on Home.

          Budget Tracker

          • Add a budget with a total amount and days. The app shows the cost per day.
          • Add expenses to track spending. A daily reminder shows your spend limit.

          Checklist

          • Create a checklist and set it Open or Closed. Open checklists show on Home.

          Priority Queue

          • Pick a date, type an item, click Add. Tick the box when done.
          • Unchecked items move to the next day automatically.

          Progress Tracker

          • Add a progress title and date, then add steps (text or a file) under it.
          • Home shows each progress title with its number of steps. Tap it to open the page.

          Piggy Bank

          • Piggy Bank page → ＋ Add New Savings → Title, Date, Amount, Notes (optional) → Save.
          • Tap ＋ Add savings under a title to add more amounts (date and amount) on the same date or any other date. "Till saved" shows next to the title.
          • Edit with ✏️, delete with 🗑️ (restore from the Trash Bin).

          Calories

          • Step 1 – Enter your weight: Calories page → ⚖️ MY WEIGHT & PROFILE. Enter your weight (kg) and Save. Weight alone is enough to calculate calories burned. Add height, age, gender and your daily routine to get your BMR (calories your body needs at rest), your maintenance calories and the weight estimate.
          • Add food: ＋ ADD FOOD → choose the meal and food type (South Indian, North Indian, Snacks, Sweets, Fruits & Nuts, Drinks – 60+ foods in each). Type in 🔍 Search to find a food across all types, then enter how many you ate (1 idli, 2 cups, ½ …). The calories are calculated for you. Use "Other" to enter your own food with the calories of one serving.
          • Add exercise: 🏃 ADD EXERCISE → pick the activity (100 activities: walking, running, cycling, gym, yoga, sports, dance, daily work) or search for it, then enter the minutes. Calories burned = activity MET × your weight × hours. Use "Other" to type calories from your watch or band.
          • Day Progress: shows eaten, burned and net calories. Your limit for the day is your goal plus the calories you burned. Use the ◀ ▶ buttons to see other days. The day balance shows how much weight that day adds or removes (about 7,700 kcal = 1 kg).
          • Weight estimate: after you finish at least one full day of food, ⚖️ MY WEIGHT & PROFILE shows your estimated weight: your last weighed weight + (calories eaten − maintenance − exercise) of every tracked day. Today is counted from tomorrow. Weigh yourself every week or two and Save the new weight to keep it accurate. It is an estimate, not medical advice.
          • Track History: last 7 days chart plus every tracked day (food and exercise). Edit with ✏️, delete with 🗑️ (restore from the Trash Bin).

          Trash Bin

          • Deleted items go to the Trash Bin. Click ♻️ to restore them.

          Settings

          • Edit Name, Dark Mode, Language, Animations, Notification Settings and Provide Feedback are inside ⚙️ Settings.
          • Click the profile picture to change it.
          • Notifications can be turned on or off for each feature. Allow notifications for this site in your browser.

          Import & Export (Backup)

          • Open ⚙️ Settings and click the 💾 storage button.
          • Export Data: downloads one backup file with all your data (reminders, notes, budgets, checklists, priority queue, progress, Piggy Bank, trash, files) and settings (name, photo, dark mode, notification choices).
          • Import Data: choose a backup file. All current data and settings are replaced by the backup and the app reloads.
          • Export regularly and before clearing browser data or changing your device. Use Import on the new device to restore everything.

          Offline Mode

          • Download the source code, extract it and open index.html without internet. Notifications will not work offline.
